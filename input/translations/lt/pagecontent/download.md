Diegimo vadovo šaltinio kodą galite atsisiųsti iš [Github](https://github.com/HL7LT/ig-lt-base).
Savo pakeitimams pasiūlyti naudokite standartinę [Pull request](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/proposing-changes-to-your-work-with-pull-requests/creating-a-pull-request) procedūrą.
